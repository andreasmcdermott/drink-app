# Catalog expansion: grenadine and orgeat

This expansion adds 20 recipes, bringing Pour to 59 recipes, including 27 gin drinks. It adds only two ingredient IDs to the existing catalog: grenadine and orgeat (almond syrup).

These counts describe catalog coverage. What a particular user can make still depends on their saved bar.

| New ingredient needed | Added recipes |
| --- | --- |
| None | Pegu Club, Fitzgerald, Gin Rickey, Bronx, Gold Rush, Hemingway Special, Champs-Élysées, Old Cuban, Garibaldi, Brandy Crusta |
| Grenadine | Monkey Gland, Scofflaw, Tequila Sunrise, El Presidente, Pink Lady, Ward Eight |
| Orgeat (almond syrup) | Army & Navy, Mai Tai, Japanese Cocktail, Trinidad Sour |

## Recipe choices

Measurements are authored in both ml and practical US measures, as elsewhere in the catalog. Garnishes are optional. Every recipe has its own illustration and matching paper color.

- Mai Tai uses one dry aged dark rum in place of the IBA's two-rum blend, plus the catalog's orange liqueur. The instructions call out the rum style.
- Old Cuban uses dry Prosecco, which the IBA recipe allows, and specifies dry aged dark rum. The sparkling wine is added after shaking.
- Monkey Gland uses restrained amounts of absinthe and grenadine, rather than the larger measures on the current IBA page.
- Pink Lady is labeled as a gin-only take; it does not claim to be the applejack version. An aquafaba variation supports egg-free matching.
- Trinidad Sour uses Angostura bitters as its base spirit, measured by volume rather than dashes. It appears in the Bitters recipe filter.
- Gold Rush specifies equal parts honey and water for the honey syrup.
