A little help.

Have a question, found a problem, or have a recipe suggestion? Get in touch.

Contact Pour

Use the project's GitHub issue tracker for questions, bug reports, recipe suggestions, and privacy questions. A GitHub account is required to post.

Send a support request ↗

Issues are public. Please leave out personal information. For a bug, include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and what you were doing. A screenshot can help if it does not show anything private.

Adding ingredients

Open My bar and tap an ingredient to add or remove it. You can also type a list, with ingredients separated by commas or “and”. Review the recognized ingredients before adding them.

If Pour does not recognize an ingredient, try a specific name such as “white rum” or “sweet vermouth”. You can use your iPhone keyboard's microphone to dictate the list.

Why isn't a drink showing as ready?

Pour checks the required ingredients against your shelf. A garnish is optional. Some recipes also have a suggested substitution that can make them work with your bar. Check My bar for anything missing or selected by mistake.

Measurements and servings

Open a recipe to switch between ml and oz or adjust the serving count. The ingredients and “Make it your own” tips update together. When making several drinks, shake or stir in small batches.

Saved ingredients and favorites

Your shelf, favorites, and measurement preference are saved on this iPhone. There is no Pour account or sync between devices. Deleting the app removes its local data, although restoring a device backup may bring it back.

To clear ingredients, use Clear my shelf in My bar. You can remove favorites using the heart on a recipe. Read the privacy policy for more about local storage and backups.

Using Pour offline

Recipes, illustrations, ingredient matching, and purchase suggestions work without an internet connection. Opening this website or contacting support requires a connection.
